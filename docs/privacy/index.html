Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Help Me Bruh is open-source software you run on your own Mac. We don't operate a server that receives your messages, email, calendar, or passwords. They stay on your Mac, except when the software sends them to services you choose to connect.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Help Me Bruh software ("Bruh"), this website, and the optional Google sign-in used to connect Gmail and Google Calendar. It is published by the Help Me Bruh project ("we"). If you run Bruh, you are the operator of your own copy.

What Bruh reads on your Mac

  • iMessages. Bruh reads messages that start with your trigger word (for example /bruh), plus a few earlier messages from the same chat for context, from the Messages database on your Mac.
  • Attachments. Photos and voice notes sent with a request, so it can describe or transcribe them.
  • Settings and history. Your settings, connected-app status, and a log of recent requests, stored in the Bruh folder on your Mac.

What leaves your Mac

Bruh sends data only to services you connect, and only to do what you asked:

  • AI model providers you choose (for example OpenAI through Codex, Vercel AI Gateway, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, or ElevenLabs) receive the request text, recent chat context, and any images or audio needed to answer.
  • Connected apps such as Google, Vercel, Netlify, or StarCraft2.ai receive the API requests needed for the task.
  • Cloudflare carries one-use sign-in links and callbacks between your phone and your Mac, if you enable phone handoff. It does not store tokens.

Each of those services handles data under its own privacy policy. We don't receive a copy.

Google user data

If you connect Gmail or Google Calendar, Bruh asks Google for read-only access: gmail.readonly to search and read your email, and calendar.readonly to check your calendar and availability. Bruh uses this data only to answer your own requests, for example "am I free tomorrow at 3?" or "any email from Alex?".

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in your Mac's Keychain. We never receive them.
  • Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. A model provider you chose may process it to produce an answer to your request.
  • People don't read your Google data, unless you ask for help with a specific message or it's required for security or by law.

Bruh's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Gmail or Calendar at any time in Bruh's settings, which deletes the local token and revokes the grant. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Passwords

If you connect a password vault (1Password or Bitwarden), Bruh fills saved logins into its browser on your Mac. Passwords are never shown to the AI model or sent in a message.

This website

helpmebruh.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may log visits as described in its own privacy statement.

Your choices

Bruh's data lives on your Mac, so you control it: change or disconnect services in Bruh's settings, delete the Bruh folder, or uninstall it. Because we don't hold your data, we can't access, export, or delete it for you.

Children

Bruh is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We'll post changes on this page and update the effective date.
